  - `target_seen_during_nav`
  - `target_seen_during_nav_image`
  - `final_visual_confirmed`
  - `active_search_used`
- Updated `src/vln_nav2_bridge/launch/vln_nav2_bridge_local.launch.py` to expose the new active-perception parameters.

Design rationale:

- The bridge now follows a more realistic modular embodied-navigation pattern:
  1. initial 360-degree scan,
  2. semantic/Nav2 navigation to a candidate region,
  3. low-frequency visual checks while moving,
  4. mandatory final scan at the candidate region,
  5. bounded local active search if final confirmation fails.
- This matches the requirement that geometry and semantics must both pass: Nav2 proves reachability, while VLM proves that the intended object is visible.
